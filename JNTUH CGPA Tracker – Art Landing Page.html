Know your CGPA, semester by semester.

A simple tracker for JNTUH students. Add subjects, pick grades, and watch your numbers update instantly.

Open the calculator

How it works

Three steps, no sign-up.

  1. Add a semester and list your subjects with their credits.
  2. Choose each grade from O to F. Grade points are looked up for you.
  3. Read your results: SGPA per semester, overall CGPA and an approximate percentage.

Features

Small, fast, private.

  • Credit-weighted SGPA and CGPA, not a plain average.
  • Multiple semesters with an SGPA trend chart.
  • Saved in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Works on phones, with automatic dark mode.

Under the hood

Plain JavaScript, on purpose.

No frameworks. Calculation logic is kept separate from the interface so it can be tested on its own.
